The additional skill icons are stored locally for the Chinese and English profile READMEs.

- Windows, GCC, Jupyter, and LabVIEW: [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon).
- Mermaid: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons).
- Origin: [OriginLab](https://www.originlab.com/) favicon, converted to PNG at its original 48 × 48 resolution.
- GeoGebra: [official SVG](https://github.com/geogebra/images/blob/main/geogebra-logo.svg), subject to [GeoGebra's icon license](https://www.geogebra.org/license).

The `-dark.svg` files adjust logo colors for readability in GitHub's dark theme. Names and marks belong to their respective owners.
